# 00 - Product North Star

CGN = Career Growth Social Life / Career Growth Social Network.

## Core promise
A student can build, prove, share and socially experience career growth in one connected system.

## Product loop
SEE -> GET INSPIRED -> DO -> PROVE -> GROW -> SHARE -> CONNECT -> DISCOVER OPPORTUNITIES -> COME BACK.

## Growth Identity
CGN should show:
- what the student is trying to become
- what they did today toward it
- proof of that work
- how skills changed over time
- consistency/streaks
- verified evidence
- expert validation
- opportunity readiness

## Product guardrails
- Home is social-first, not dashboard-first.
- My Career is execution-first.
- 180-day planning is one important feature, not the entire product.
- AI remains mostly invisible and supports planning, adaptation, recommendation, evidence assistance and matching.
- Avoid generic motivational-feed behavior.

## V2 approved product direction — 2026-10-16 (CGN-288)

The founder reports team approval of **CGN Student Ecosystem V2**: one continuous, opt-in educational, exam-preparation, social and career-growth journey from after Class 10 through college and professional life. One student identity may hold multiple evolving goals/stages; prior meaningful evidence and appropriate existing connections persist with privacy controls.

**Existing core loop remains unchanged**, but personalized by education stage, goals and voluntary sharing. Home remains social-first; execution workspace can be labeled "My Journey" in future approved designs, while existing code/API ownership stays unchanged. AI remains support tooling rather than canonical exam, qualification or career truth. Pilot NEET + B.Tech is a **hypothesis pending CGN-292 student validation**, not a shipped feature.

**Authoritative scope documents:** [CGN V2 Product Vision](../product/CGN_V2_PRODUCT_VISION.md) and [gap audit](../product/CGN_V2_FEATURE_GAP_AUDIT.md). **Engineering change gate:** [CGN-290](https://sandeepagrahari1.atlassian.net/browse/CGN-290) must review schemas/contracts/age safeguards; [CGN-291](https://sandeepagrahari1.atlassian.net/browse/CGN-291) owns reviewed Figma screens. This approved product *direction* does **not** certify changed APIs, implemented student-preparation features, approved Figma frames or public launch.

**New guardrails:** no compulsive peer connections, forced public score comparisons, manipulative FOMO alerts, unsupported mentor credentials or unaudited contacts involving minors. Consent, role/age policies and verified evidence apply across Feed, Social Graph, Communities, Messaging, Experts and Notifications.
