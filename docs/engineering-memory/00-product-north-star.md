# 00 - Product North Star

CGN = Career Growth Social Life / Career Growth Social Network.

## Core promise
A student can build, prove, share and socially experience career growth in one connected system.

## Product loop
SEE -> GET INSPIRED -> DO -> PROVE -> GROW -> SHARE -> CONNECT -> DISCOVER OPPORTUNITIES -> COME BACK.

## Growth Identity
CGN should show:
- what the student is trying to become
- what they did today toward it
- proof of that work
- how skills changed over time
- consistency/streaks
- verified evidence
- expert validation
- opportunity readiness

## Product guardrails
- Home is social-first, not dashboard-first.
- My Career is execution-first.
- 180-day planning is one important feature, not the entire product.
- AI remains mostly invisible and supports planning, adaptation, recommendation, evidence assistance and matching.
- Avoid generic motivational-feed behavior.
